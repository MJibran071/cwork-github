# Cwork Community Guidelines

**Last Updated:** September 18, 2025

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to the Cwork community! These guidelines are designed to ensure a safe, professional, and productive environment for all users. By using Cwork, you agree to abide by these guidelines.

## 2. Core Principles

### 2.1 Professional Conduct
- Treat all users with respect and professionalism
- Communicate clearly and constructively
- Maintain a positive and collaborative atmosphere

### 2.2 Platform Security
- Keep all communications and transactions within the Cwork platform
- Use the built-in messaging and contract systems for all project-related discussions
- Report any suspicious activity immediately

## 3. Prohibited Behaviors

### 3.1 Contact Information Exchange
**Strictly Prohibited:** Users must not exchange personal contact information (including email addresses, phone numbers, social media handles, or any off-platform communication methods) before a formal contract has been established through the Cwork platform.

**Why this rule exists:**
- Ensures all communications are monitored for safety and quality
- Maintains a secure environment for dispute resolution
- Protects users from potential scams or unsafe interactions
- Allows proper moderation of all platform interactions

### 3.2 Other Prohibited Activities
- Harassment, discrimination, or hate speech
- Spamming or unsolicited commercial messages
- Sharing inappropriate or offensive content
- Attempting to circumvent platform fees or systems
- Impersonation or false representation

## 4. Communication Standards

### 4.1 Pre-Contract Communications
- All initial discussions must occur through Cwork's messaging system
- Keep conversations professional and project-focused
- Do not pressure other users to move communications off-platform

### 4.2 Post-Contract Communications
- Even after contract establishment, we recommend keeping project communications on-platform for record-keeping and dispute resolution
- Use platform tools for file sharing, video calls, and collaboration

## 5. Moderation and Enforcement

### 5.1 Automated Monitoring
Cwork employs automated systems to detect:
- Attempted contact information exchange
- Suspicious patterns of behavior
- Potential policy violations

### 5.2 Human Review
All flagged communications are subject to human review by our moderation team.

### 5.3 Consequences for Violations
Violations may result in:
- Warning notifications
- Temporary suspension of messaging privileges
- Permanent account termination
- Legal action in severe cases

## 6. Reporting Mechanisms

### 6.1 How to Report
- Use the "Report" button on any message or user profile
- Contact support through the help center
- Provide detailed information about the violation

### 6.2 Anonymous Reporting
Users can report violations anonymously while maintaining privacy.

## 7. Appeal Process

If you believe action was taken in error, you may:
1. Submit an appeal through the support system
2. Provide additional context or evidence
3. Receive a response within 48 hours

## 8. Continuous Improvement

These guidelines evolve based on:
- Community feedback
- Changing security needs
- Platform development
- Industry best practices

## 9. Contact Information

For questions about these guidelines or to report violations:
- Use the in-platform support system
- Check the FAQ section
- Review our Terms of Service for additional policies

---

**By participating in the Cwork community, you agree to follow these guidelines and contribute to maintaining a safe, professional environment for all users.**
